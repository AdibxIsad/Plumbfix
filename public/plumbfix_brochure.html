PROBLEM STATEMENT
  • Manual Booking Bottlenecks: Reliance on WhatsApp & calls causes lost inquiries, missed bookings, and scheduling conflicts.
  • No Service Rating Platform: Absence of structured customer feedback tracking makes evaluating plumber performance difficult.
  • Poor Data & History Tracking: Paper-based logs lead to lost service records and poor repair history tracking for returning clients.
AIM

To develop a web-based plumbing management system for Ikhlas Jujur Bakti that enables direct customer booking, automates deposit verification, streamlines technician dispatching, and eliminates response delays.

OBJECTIVES
  • To gather and analyze business and functional requirements for Ikhlas Jujur Bakti's plumbing management system.
  • To design the Plumbfix web portal using storyboards and standard UML diagrams based on requirements.
  • To develop and implement the web-based plumbing management system for customers, plumbers, and administrators.

ABOUT SYSTEM

IKHLAS JUJUR BAKTI

Premier Plumbing Services • Bangi, Selangor

In 2018, Ikhlas Jujur Bakti was established as a plumbing service enterprise in Bangi, Selangor, providing residential and commercial pipe repairs and maintenance. According to co-owner Mr. Ahmad Shariati, customer bookings were previously managed through manual WhatsApp messages and phone calls to enquire about pricing and slot availability. Staff manually verified deposit payments and scheduled plumbers, leading to response delays and booking conflicts. To address this, Plumbfix was developed as a web-based platform to automate bookings, deposit verifications, live technician chat, and job history tracking.

Core Platform Capabilities:

  • Customers: Real-time booking, deposit upload, live plumber chat, & rating system.
  • Plumbers: Job logging, materials/labor tracking, photo proof upload, & PDF invoice generation.
  • Admins: Deposit verifications, refund tickets, plumber dispatch, & demand analytics.
Tech Stack: Laravel 11 • Tailwind CSS v4 • MySQL • Pusher WebSockets
