PLUMBFIX: PLUMBING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

FOR IKHLAS JUJUR BAKTI
INTRODUCTION

The Plumbfix: Plumbing Management System is a web-based portal developed for Ikhlas Jujur Bakti to modernize plumbing service bookings, technician dispatching, deposit verification via DuitNow QR, real-time live chat communication, and automated data tracking & analytics. It enhances operational efficiency, eliminates lost service records, and delivers a 24/7 digital booking experience.

SMART PORTAL
PROBLEM STATEMENT
Manual Booking Process

Scheduling relies on fragmented phone calls and chat messages, leading to scheduling conflicts, human errors, and delayed technician dispatches.

No Feedback Platform

Absence of a dedicated feedback and rating system makes evaluating technician performance and assuring customer service quality impossible.

Poor Record & Data Tracking

Operational logs and deposit receipts are unorganized, causing lost records and difficulty in generating financial and revenue reports.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES
1
Requirement Analysis

To gather and analyze functional & non-functional requirements for the Plumbfix: Plumbing Management System.

2
Web Portal Design

To design the web-based portal architecture, ERD, and user interfaces for Ikhlas Jujur Bakti.

3
System Development

To develop and test the Plumbfix system featuring real-time booking, payment verification, and analytics.

METHODOLOGY
1. Requirement & Analysis

Gathered business workflow specs for Ikhlas Jujur Bakti & defined SRS.

2. System Design

Designed ERD, Sequence diagrams, Data Dictionary & UI wireframes.

3. System Development

Built full-stack portal using Laravel 12, Tailwind CSS & MySQL.

4. Testing & Evaluation

Executed functional tests, payment verification & User Acceptance Testing.

PROJECT SIGNIFICANCE
FOR STAFF / BUSINESS
  • Replaces manual chat bookings, zero schedule overlaps.
  • Centralized deposit verification & technician dispatching.
  • Automated sales reports to track revenue & trends.
FOR CUSTOMERS
  • 24/7 self-service booking portal without waiting for replies.
  • Real-time live chat with plumbers & PDF receipts.
  • Direct customer ratings & feedback submission.
CONCLUSION

The Plumbfix: Plumbing Management System successfully modernizes the traditional plumbing service workflow for Ikhlas Jujur Bakti into a centralized web application. By automating order bookings, payment verification, live chat, and reporting, the system significantly improves operational efficiency for staff while delivering a seamless, 24/7 ordering experience for customers.
